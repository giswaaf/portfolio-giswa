Freshmen Partner

A volunteer role at Bina Nusantara University focused on supporting incoming students as they begin their university experience.

RoleVolunteer
OrganizationBina Nusantara University

Welcoming new students

As a Freshmen Partner, I take part in the university community and help create a more welcoming transition for incoming students. The role gives me an opportunity to share the perspective of a fellow student and be a point of support.

Communication and community

This experience helps me practice clear communication, empathy, and collaboration while building connections across the BINUS student community.

My contribution

Serving as a Freshmen Partner taught me how to balance empathetic mentorship with structured team leadership. Guiding first-year students through their academic and campus transition sharpened my active listening, interpersonal communication, and problem-solving skills when answering diverse questions and resolving group challenges. Additionally, leading their team projects strengthened my ability to delegate tasks, coordinate timelines, and keep members motivated toward shared goals.
